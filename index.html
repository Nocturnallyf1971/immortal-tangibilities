Our Story

Crafted from love,
born from loss

We are a mother and daughter duo — struggling artists who found purpose in our own grief. After losing our beloved Megan JoLynn on Christmas 2016, we discovered that creating something tangible was the most healing act we could offer ourselves — and others.

Every piece we create is infused with intention, empathy, and the understanding that grief is not a wound to be closed, but a love that needs somewhere to live.

We work closely with each client through a personal consultation, listening to the story of their loved one and crafting a keepsake that honors that unique bond — whether through jewelry, amulets, or memorial objects made with our own hands.

✦   Handcrafted with Care

Every piece is made entirely by hand — no mass production, no shortcuts. Each item carries the energy and intention of two artists who understand loss deeply.

✦   Personal Consultation

We sit with you and listen to the story of your loved one. Together, we choose the right keepsake to honor their memory and support your healing journey.

✦   Dedicated to Megan JoLynn

This work is dedicated to our Megan, whose absence inspired us to transform grief into art — and art into healing for others who carry the same ache.

Book a Consultation

Begin your
healing journey

Our consultation is a sacred, unhurried conversation. We want to hear about your loved one — their spirit, their essence, the things that made them irreplaceable.

From there, we guide you in choosing a keepsake that feels true to your bond and supports wherever you are in your grief.

01

Submit your request and we'll reach out within 2–3 days to schedule your personal session.

02

We meet (in person or virtually) and you share the story of your loved one at your own pace.

03

Together we choose or design your keepsake, and we begin crafting it with love and intention.

04

Your handcrafted piece is delivered to you — a tangible piece of immortal love.

This is a safe, compassionate space. There is no pressure — only care.
